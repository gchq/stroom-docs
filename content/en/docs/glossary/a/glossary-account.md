---
title: "Account"
linkTitle: "Account"
description: >
  Refers to a user account in Stroom's internal Identity Provider.
  An Account holds the credentials a person authenticates with, and exists only where Stroom is its own Identity Provider, unlike a _User_ which exists in every deployment.
